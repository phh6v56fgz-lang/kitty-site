How to Adopt

  1. Join the waitlist. Call, text, or email us and tell us a little about your home.
  2. Hear from us. We'll reach out when kittens are available.
  3. Pick your kitty. We'll help you find a good personality match for your family.
  4. Pickup. Bring your new family member home from West Valley City, UT.

Questions about pricing, deposits, and our policies? Just ask. We're happy to go over everything with you before you decide.
